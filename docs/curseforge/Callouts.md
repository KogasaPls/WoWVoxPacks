# WoWVoxPacks Callouts ({Voice})

A TTS voice pack of raid callouts. Several hundred recordings of raid cooldowns and mechanics in the Google Cloud **{Voice}** voice ({VoiceDescription}), added to the sound dropdown of every addon that shares one.

No configuration is required, just install and load the addon. The sounds are listed as `WVP {Voice}: ...`, in orange near the bottom of the dropdown, and nothing plays until you pick one.

## Where you can use it

Any addon whose sound dropdown reads from LibSharedMedia, including:

- BigWigs, as a custom sound for an ability
- Northern Sky Raid Tools, in a sound field
- Liquid Reminders, in a reminder template

## What is in it

Raid cooldown spell names (`Tranquility`, `Rallying Cry`, `Anti-Magic Zone`), some common abbreviations (`Tranq`, `Rally`, `AMZ`, `BoP`, `Lust`, `PI`), and some common raid callouts (`Soak Skull`, `Frontal`, `Interrupt`, `Spread`).

## Requirements

LibSharedMedia-3.0, which comes bundled with almost every raid addon, and something to play the sounds from.

## Which WoWVoxPacks addon do I want?

| Pack | Use it when | Voices at once |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [BigWigs Voice]({Url:BigWigs_Voice}) | you want BigWigs to speak boss ability names | one enabled |
| [BigWigs Countdown]({Url:BigWigs_Countdown}) | you want the BigWigs countdown in this voice | as many as you like |
| [Northern Sky Raid Tools]({Url:NorthernSkyRaidTools}) | you use NSRT and want its callouts to stop going through the in-game text-to-speech | one |
| [Callouts]({Url:Callouts}) | you want to pick voice lines by hand in BigWigs, NSRT or Liquid Reminders | as many as you like |
| [ExBoss]({Url:ExBoss}) | you use ExBoss and want its callouts spoken in English | as many as you like |

## Voices

Wavenet_E (female), Neural2_C (female) and Studio_Q (male). Samples are on the [GitHub page](https://github.com/KogasaPls/WoWVoxPacks#voices). Install as many of them as you like; they sit side by side.

## Source

Generated from Google Cloud Text-to-Speech by [WoWVoxPacks](https://github.com/KogasaPls/WoWVoxPacks), Apache-2.0. Mispronunciations and missing callouts are worth an issue there.
